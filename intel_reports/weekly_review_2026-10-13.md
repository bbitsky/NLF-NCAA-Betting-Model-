## What Worked / What Didn't

1. **Derived Unders carried the week.** CHI team total Under 23.25, TB team total Under 18, GB@TB 1H Under 19.5, MIA@MIN 1H Under 19.5, and NYJ@CHI Under 43 all won; derived is 5-0 this week and 20-10-2 (+8.18u) season. The underlying games finished 3.5 (IND/WAS), 8 (GB/TB), 8 (NYJ/CHI) and 13 (MIA/MIN) points under their closing totals, all QB-downgrade or skill-out games.
2. **Spreads were the leak, again.** 0-2-1 on card spreads (PIT -2.5 soft lean lost by 3, WAS +5 lost by 17, GB -3 pushed). Season spreads are still +2.36u, but both losses were low-conviction C leans on injury/QB narratives where the market had already moved (WAS +5 was 0.5 worse than model entry; the row itself said so).
3. **QB-change Unders are not a blanket rule.** IND/WAS (43 total vs 46.5), GB/TB (31 vs 39), NYJ/CHI (35 vs 43), MIA/MIN (25 vs 38) all went Under, but ARI/NYG (Dart out, Winston in) went 60 vs 44.5 and ATL/NO went 69 vs 48. The Unders hit where both offenses lost skill/QB talent and the line had not fully moved; where one side kept a competent offense and the other had a defensive collapse, it went Over. n=6, early.
4. **Forced picks lost mostly on a bad week for favorites, not a bad process.** 11 of 13 FP spreads backed the same team as the SU pick and went 2-8-1; favorites as a group were 4-10-2 ATS, 9-7 SU. Without a price edge, the "pick the winner and the cover" approach equals roughly a coin flip minus juice in a normal week and worse in a dog week.
5. **SU picks at -110 hide the price.** 9-6 looks like +2.18u at -110 flat, but at the user's actual moneylines it is about -2.2u. The best SU pick type was 60-69% (5-1); the <60% coin-flip bucket was 1-4, with ARI, CIN, HOU and NO all losing outright.
6. **Props split 1-1 and the role rule held.** Bowers (incumbent 92% route share, 20-yd TD) hit; Barner (goal-line role) missed; LAC's only TDs were Vidal and Hampton. Props are 3-8, -5.27u season.

2. **FP spreads stay a separate bucket and do not feed tiers.** Report FP same-team vs split each week; do not draw any rule from one dog-heavy week (n=13).
3. **Cap Tier C spread leans built on a QB/injury narrative to 1 per card.** The `injury` tag is 8-17 (-9.73u) season and the two Week 4 spread losses were narrative leans where the market had already moved.
4. **Keep derived Under expressions (team total, 1H total) only when both offenses are impaired and the full-game total has not already been cut by 5+ points.** Log ARI/NYG and ATL/NO as counterexamples.
5. **1H favorite lean rule:** the "at or below half-spread" rule stays, but the supporting base rate is now 52.5% (n=61), not 55.6%. Treat 1H favorite leans as PASS unless the price is below half the spread by 1+ point. (Data-based edit; see learnings.)
