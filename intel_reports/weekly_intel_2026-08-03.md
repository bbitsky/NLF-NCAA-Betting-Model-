### NCAAF — transfer portal

The 2026 cycle was the **first without a spring window**. Portal entry was restricted to Jan 2–16, so rosters have been effectively locked since mid-January. The only live exception path is a coaching change outside the window, which opens a 15-day window (e.g., Northern Illinois after Thomas Hammock left for the NFL).

**Implication for the model:** unlike prior seasons, there is no August roster churn in NCAAF. Preseason ratings built in June are still valid personnel-wise — only injury and eligibility news should move them.

Notable completed portal classes: **LSU** (Lane Kiffin — aggressive, "ultimate free-agent experiment" roster), **Texas Tech** (landed Brendan Sorsby, top-ranked QB on the market, 3-year starter, dual-threat), **Indiana** (Cignetti prioritized starting experience around a new QB).

The NCAA FBS Oversight Committee has **proposed a new portal window structure** — no action yet, but it affects 2027 modeling.
