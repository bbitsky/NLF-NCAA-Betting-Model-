## What Worked / What Didn't

Tier A had its best week yet (4-1, +2.64u) and both of its total picks (W2-13 Under 44.5 at 64.7% confidence, W2-30 Under 47.7 at 56.0%) hit — the card's highest-conviction reads are earning that conviction so far, 8-3 combined across two weeks.

The 16:05/16:25 ET kickoff window is now a season-long net loser (12-14-1, -3.09u) and produced a 7-game losing streak in Week 2 alone. This isn't yet enough data to indict the window itself (it's disproportionately Tier C, which is inherently closer to coin-flip by construction), but it's the single most visible structural pattern in two weeks of data and deserves a dedicated look before Week 3.

The prop market is 2-4 for -2.18u across two weeks, and three of Week 2's five prop rows were shipped with TBD lines that could never be graded to a number (Montgomery rush yards, Mayer receptions, Jones passing yards) — the exact failure mode flagged after Week 1 ("don't ship a prop row with a TBD line") recurred instead of being fixed. This is a process gap, not a variance story.

A pre-game QB-availability read was wrong in a way that mattered: the card built five separate Week 2 rows (W2-25, W2-26, W2-H2, W2-P4, W2-P5) around "Mac Jones starts, Purdy out/unlikely" for MIA@SF — Purdy actually started and played the entire game. The spread/total picks on that game both lost regardless of which QB played (SF just won bigger than expected), but the specific prop (Jones passing yards) and the specific rationale on four other rows were built on a premise that didn't hold by kickoff. This is the same class of problem the standing "re-check QB status the morning of" rule already targets — it just wasn't caught here because none of these rows were built or re-checked close enough to kickoff.

## Adjustments for Next Card

Do not ship a prop row with a TBD price/line unless it is a binary anytime-TD market (which doesn't need a number to grade). This rule already existed after Week 1 and was violated three times in Week 2 — either get a real number before the card locks, drop the row, or reclassify it as a directional note in the daily intel instead of a card row.

Add a same-day (Sunday-morning) QB-active check specifically for any row whose rationale names a backup QB by name, not just a "questionable/doubtful" tag — the MIA@SF miss came from a "toe, questionable/unlikely" read that had flipped to "active and started" by kickoff, and it drove five card rows.

Flag the 16:05/16:25 ET window for closer review before Week 3 — not a hard rule change yet (n=27 season-to-date, and the tier mix is skewed), but worth tracking as its own line item going forward the way kickoff-window is already broken out here.
