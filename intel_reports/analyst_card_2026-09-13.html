How to read this. Each call is the side or total the data favors and why. "Model conf" is the model's win probability — a prior, not a guarantee. The real edge is the board number vs. the number the model entered at: better than entry is an upgrade, worse is a downgrade, and crossing 3 or 7 matters more than any half-point elsewhere. Tiers: A model ≥55% and number at/better than entry with no unpriced injury · B strong model but worse number, or sharp-money agreement on a weaker number · C at entry, small confidence, or unmodeled injury risk · PASS no data-supported side.
