I'm not treating 54.5% model confidence as meaningfully different from a market-driven read; it's one more data point pointing the same direction as the line move, not a standalone edge.

If you're pricing this off the model alone, most of the value that existed at -4.5 is now gone — you'd be laying an extra point to bet the side the model liked three days ago. The case for Buffalo right now rests on the reverse-line-movement read (sharp money still active into kickoff), not on the model retaining an edge at the current number.
