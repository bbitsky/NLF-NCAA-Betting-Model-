3. **Early-week weather flags keep not panning out** — DAL@NYG (rain, no material impact per recaps) and now DEN@KC (wind/precip flagged early in the week, nothing material found on the day-of intel scan) both suggest the process should recheck weather same-day before it factors into a pick, not rely on a multi-day-old forecast.
4. **All three "no lean stated" PASSes (X1, X2, X5) were genuinely unresolvable, and the process is working as designed** — the model declined to guess rather than force a side once qb-change, coin-flip, or "no pick in ledger" conditions applied. That's a good sign for card discipline, not a gap to fix.
5. **Props were a coin flip (2-2)** and both NOGRADEs (Etienne rush yards, Evans/Egbuka receptions) came from lines that were never actually established at card time (TBD/TBD). That's a process gap, not a model miss — see adjustments below.

- **Stop shipping props with a TBD line.** W1-P2 and W1-P6 were unrated cards from the start — either get a real number before the card locks or don't put the row on the card. Two dead rows out of 28 is a real cost.

If a weather tag is going to influence a total lean, it needs a day-of confirmation, not a standing flag from the daily intel a few days out.
