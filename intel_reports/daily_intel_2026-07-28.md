**Vita Vea requests trade from Tampa Bay (contract standoff).** The two-time Pro Bowl DT, 31, entering the final year of a 4-year/$71M deal ($17M in 2026, none guaranteed), asked out after extension talks collapsed. This is the single most line-relevant health item in the league — a cleared Mahomes props up the Chiefs' number (currently ~+1800 to win SB61) and every KC spread in the first six weeks. If he practices without setback through the first week of August, the market has already priced it; the edge was in buying before clearance, not after.
