# NFL + NCAAF Daily Intel — August 2, 2026

## 🚨 BREAKING / HIGH IMPACT

**49ers WR Ricky Pearsall out for the season.** San Francisco confirmed Pearsall will undergo knee surgery and miss all of 2026 — the lingering PCL issue from last season never resolved and knee swelling kept him out of camp practices. This is the single biggest line-moving item on the board today. Combined with **Christian Kirk's calf strain** (announced Monday, from Day 1 of camp), the 49ers' WR room behind free-agent addition Mike Evans is suddenly thin, with Stribling projected into a starting role. Watch SF's win total (currently sitting in the 9.5–10 range) and the Week 1 Melbourne line vs. the Rams for a half-point-plus move over the next 48 hours.

**Panthers EDGE Nic Scourton to IR — torn ACL, right knee.** Suffered Day 1 of camp. Carolina's pass rush was already a question mark, and this lands four days before they play the Hall of Fame Game. Panthers win total (currently ~6.5) and Week 1 vs. Chicago (CHI -2.5) both look vulnerable to a downtick.

**Falcons QB room is compromised.** Kevin Stefanski confirmed Tua Tagovailoa will miss early portions of camp with an undisclosed issue, and Michael Penix Jr. still has not been cleared for full team activities coming off last year's Week 11 ACL tear. Atlanta signed Cooper Rush as insurance after waiving Trevor Siemian with an NFI designation. Two damaged QBs and a career backup is not a Week 1 recipe — Atlanta's win total is a live under candidate if Tua's absence extends past mid-August.
