## What Worked / What Didn't

1. **Team-total "consistent expression" bets were the best market on the card (4-1, +2.64u)** — even when their listed full-game total parent lost (W1-T1, ARI over the implied 18.75 despite the Over47 total missing), because the *spread* half of the expression carried it. Worth keeping as a distinct market, not folding into total performance.
2. **"Worse number than the model wanted" still went 2-1 this week (DAL-3, ARI+9.5, CHI-3)** — small sample, but a reminder that model-entry-vs-taken-number deltas of half a point rarely decide a game; margin swings this week (11-, 15-, 19-, 49-point games) dwarfed any half-point of shopping.
3. **The weather tag did nothing useful this week** — both weather-tagged rows trace back to the same DAL@NYG game, and recaps explicitly said rain had no material impact. One game isn't a test of the thesis either way.
4. **Both qb-change PASSes stayed genuinely unresolved** — the model correctly recognized it had no valid number once Rush and the Bijan-script uncertainty entered the picture, and declined to guess. That's the system working as designed, not a gap to fix.
5. **Props were a coin flip (2-2)** and both NOGRADEs (Etienne rush yards, Evans/Egbuka receptions) came from lines that were never actually established at card time (TBD/TBD). That's a process gap, not a model miss — see adjustments below.

- **Stop shipping props with a TBD line.** W1-P2 and W1-P6 were unrated cards from the start — either get a real number before the card locks or don't put the row on the card. Two dead rows out of 27 is a real cost.
