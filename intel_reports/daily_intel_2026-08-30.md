# NFL + NCAAF Daily Intel — 2026-08-30

## 🚨 BREAKING / HIGH IMPACT
**53-man roster cutdown deadline is TODAY, Sunday Aug 30 at 6:00 PM ET** — the biggest single day on the pre-season calendar. All 32 teams are trimming from 90-man camp rosters to 53, meaning 1,100+ players will be released, waived, or reverted to IR before kickoff. The deadline was moved from the usual Tuesday to Sunday this year because the 49ers fly to Melbourne, Australia this week for their Sept. 10 opener vs. the Rams — squeezing the Bears and Titans (who play their final preseason game Saturday night) into a sub-24-hour cutdown window. Depth charts across the league will shift materially over the next 24-48 hours as practice-squad and waiver-claim moves follow. This is the single highest-impact news day before Week 1 lines take shape — re-scan tomorrow for post-cut roster and depth chart fallout.

Other notable moves today: the Cowboys traded for former first-round OT Broderick Jones (from the Steelers, for a pair of draft picks); the Dolphins traded QB Quinn Ewers to the Jaguars; Seahawks DT Leonard Williams agreed to a 3-year, $90M extension; the Commanders released K Jake Moody (19/23 on FGs last season); the Browns named Watson the starting QB over Sanders (coach says it's "not a week-by-week thing").
