    About the "fixed" idea: nothing in this data shows rigging. Lines move for injuries and money, and big favorites win outright about 75-80% of the time, so roughly 1 in 4 heavy favorites loses. That is normal. The sportsbook's edge is the juice (-110 on both sides), not the results. Public bettors are 41-36-2 against the spread this season, which is a coin flip. Upset betting works only when the price is better than the real chance, so size small.
